Setup

Setup steps

  1. Place the device on a stable, level surface.
  2. Plug the power connector into the rear of the device. Connect one end of the power cord into the power supply unit and the other end into a power outlet.
  3. Connect the air tubing firmly to the air outlet located on the rear of the device.
  4. Open the humidifier and fill it with water up to the maximum water level mark.
  5. Close the humidifier and insert it into the side of the device.
  6. Connect the free end of the air tubing firmly onto the assembled mask.

For detailed information about the mask, refer to the mask user guide. Recommended masks are available from the manufacturer.

⚠️ CAUTION

  • Do not overfill the humidifier. Water may enter the device and air tubing.
  • Fill the humidifier only up to the maximum water level mark.
  • Do not fill the humidifier with hot water.
