6. Power Save Mode

The AirSense 10 device records your therapy data. To allow this data to be transmitted to your care provider, you should not unplug the device.

You can use power save mode to save electricity while keeping the device plugged in.

To enter power save mode

  • Press and hold Start/Stop for three seconds.
  • The screen goes black.

To exit power save mode

  • Press Start/Stop once.
  • The Home screen is displayed.
