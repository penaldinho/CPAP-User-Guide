Starting therapy

4. Starting Therapy

Steps

  1. Fit your mask.
  2. Press Start/Stop, or breathe normally if SmartStart™ is enabled.

How you know therapy is on

  • The Sleep Report screen is displayed.
  • The current treatment pressure is shown in green.
  • During ramp time the pressure is gradually increasing and you will see a spinning circle. Once the prescribed treatment pressure is reached, the entire circle will be green.

Screen behaviour

  • The screen will go black automatically after a short period of time.
  • Press Home or the dial to turn the screen back on.

Additional information

  • If power is interrupted during therapy, the device will automatically restart therapy when power is restored.
  • The device has a light sensor that adjusts screen brightness based on the light in the room.
