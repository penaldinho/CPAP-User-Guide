⚠️ Note

The Elbow is permanently attached to the Frame . It is not intended to be removed from the mask by the home user. If removed unintentionally, refer to the image below.

Advanced Assembly

To assemble Elbow back into Frame:

  • Position the Elbow so it is facing upward.
  • Push the Elbow at an angle and rotate into the Frame until the Elbow pops in.
