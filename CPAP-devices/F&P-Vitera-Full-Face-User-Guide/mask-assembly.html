6. Mask Assembly

Reassembling Your Mask

  1. Push the Seal firmly into place on the Frame .
  2. Push the Swivel onto the Elbow .
  3. Slide the Forehead Clip onto the top of the Frame .
  4. Hook the Headgear Clips onto the Frame .

💡 Mask Assembly Tip

Check the Swivel is not stuck in the CPAP tube.
