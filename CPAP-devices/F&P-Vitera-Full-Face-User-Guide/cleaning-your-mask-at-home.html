5. Cleaning Your Mask at Home

Disassembly for Cleaning

  1. Unhook the Headgear Clips from the Frame .
  2. Slide the Forehead Clip downwards towards the Seal .
  3. Remove the Seal from the Frame .
  4. Remove the Swivel from the Elbow .

Cleaning Your Mask at Home

Wash your mask (excluding the Headgear ) after each use:

  1. Hand-wash your mask in soap dissolved in lukewarm water. Do not soak for more than 10 minutes.
  2. Rinse thoroughly in fresh water, ensuring that all soap residue has been removed.
  3. Inspect all parts to ensure they are visually clean. If necessary, repeat the cleaning steps until all parts are visually clean.
  4. Leave all parts to dry out of direct sunlight before reassembling.

Wash your Headgear after 7 days use:

  1. Hand-wash your Headgear in soap dissolved in lukewarm water. Do not soak for more than 10 minutes.
  2. Rinse thoroughly in fresh water, ensuring that all soap residue has been removed.
  3. Inspect all parts to ensure they are visually clean. If necessary, repeat the cleaning steps until all parts are visually clean.
  4. Leave all parts to dry out of direct sunlight before reassembling.

⚠️ Cleaning Cautions

  • It is not necessary to remove the Headgear Clips or the Forehead Clip when cleaning the Headgear .
  • Do not remove the Elbow from the Frame . It is not intended to be detached by the home user.
  • Do not pull on or attempt to remove the Silicone Flap from the Elbow , or attempt to disassemble the Elbow. It is not intended to be disassembled.
  • Do not clean your mask in a dishwasher.
  • Do not clean your mask with products containing alcohol, antibacterial agents, antiseptic, bleach, chlorine or moisturizer.
  • Do not store your mask in direct sunlight.
  • Do not iron the Headgear .

The above actions may deteriorate or damage the mask and shorten its life. If there is any visible deterioration do not use your mask and seek replacement part(s).
